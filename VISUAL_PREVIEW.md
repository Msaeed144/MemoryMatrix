## 🎨 Color Scheme

### Background
- **Gradient**: Purple to violet
- **Start color**: #667eea (Light purple)
- **End color**: #764ba2 (Deep violet)
- **Direction**: Diagonal (135 degrees)

### Logo Cards
- **Background**: Pure white (#ffffff)
- **Border**: Rounded (20px radius)
- **Shadow**: Soft drop shadow
- **Size**: 120x120px each

### Title
- **Font**: Pacifico (cursive, playful)
- **Color**: White
- **Size**: 4rem (very large)
- **Effect**: Text shadow, pulse animation

### Game Buttons
- **Background**: White
- **Text**: Dark gray (#333)
- **Icon color**: Purple (#667eea)
- **Size**: 280-400px wide
- **Shape**: Rounded (25px radius)
- **Shadow**: Medium drop shadow

## ✨ Animations You'll See

### On Page Load:
1. **Logos** slide down from top (1 second)
2. **Title** fades in with pulse effect (0.8 seconds)
3. **Left button** slides in from left (0.8 seconds)
4. **Right button** slides in from right (0.8 seconds)
5. **Footer** fades in last (1.5 seconds)

### On Hover (Mouse Over Buttons):
1. Button **lifts up** (10px)
2. Button **scales slightly** (5% larger)
3. Shadow gets **stronger**
4. Icon **rotates** 180 degrees
5. Icon **changes color** to purple
6. Background **gradient overlay** appears

### On Logo Hover:
1. Logo card **lifts up** (5px)
2. Logo **rotates** slightly (2 degrees)
3. Shadow gets **stronger**

### Continuous Animations:
1. Title gently **pulses** (scales 1-1.02)
2. Gradient background is **static but beautiful**
